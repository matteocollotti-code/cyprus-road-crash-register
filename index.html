How the rows are built

  • Each row is one article. When several outlets report the same crash, the earliest listed article stands as the record and every later one carries * in Duplicate Flag. Tick “Hide duplicates” to count crashes rather than articles.
  • Day, Month and Year are the date of the crash when the article gives or implies it (“on Saturday night”), otherwise the publication date.
  • Severity is Fatal when a person died at the scene or later of crash injuries, and every article about that crash carries the same severity. When a post-mortem found the only death was from a medical cause, such as a heart attack at the wheel, the crash is Non-Fatal and the outcome says so.
  • A block of 41 Cyprus Times posts carries a broken publication date (all stamped 2 June 2016), so those articles are left out.
  • Only press reports are used. Police and government statistics portals are left out, and so are crashes in the north of the island outside the Republic’s effective control.
  • Facebook pages of Cyprus outlets cannot be read reliably without signing in, so the register cites the outlets’ own articles, which their Facebook posts link to.
  • Exports follow the filters and order you have set. Excel files keep the links clickable.
